5 UAE art exhibitions to see in November, from Louvre Abu Dhabi to Sharjah. Save this for your next gallery day.

1. Art Here 2026: Confluences, Louvre Abu Dhabi. Six artists from India, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Malaysia on migration and the Indian Ocean, curated by Kamini Sawhney. Until 28 February 2027
2. Shaikha Al Mazrou: Two Horizons, Cultural Foundation, Abu Dhabi. Steel, marble, glass and copper that don't behave the way you expect. Until 18 July 2027
3. Paul Guiragossian: 100 Years of Art, Bassam Freiha Art Foundation, Abu Dhabi. His 1948 self-portrait is shown outside Lebanon for the first time. Until 17 January 2027
4. Vantage Point 13, Sharjah Art Foundation. Six photographers, all new commissions. Until 29 November
5. Provisional Sanctuaries, Tashkeel, Dubai. Shamin Sharum on the small prayer rooms in UAE warehouses and workplaces. Until 27 November

Two of these close in November, so don't leave them too late.
