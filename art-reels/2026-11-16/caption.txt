5 UAE art exhibitions to see in November, from Louvre Abu Dhabi to Sharjah. Save this for your next gallery day.

1. Art Here 2026: Confluences at @louvreabudhabi. Six artists from India, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Malaysia on migration and the Indian Ocean, curated by Kamini Sawhney. Until 28 February 2027
2. Shaikha Al Mazrou: Two Horizons at the Cultural Foundation @abudhabicf, Abu Dhabi. Steel, marble, glass and copper that don't behave the way you expect. Until 18 July 2027
3. Paul Guiragossian: 100 Years of Art at @bassamfreihaartfoundation, Abu Dhabi. His 1948 self-portrait is shown outside Lebanon for the first time. Until 17 January 2027
4. Vantage Point 13 at @sharjahart. Six photographers, all new commissions. Until 29 November
5. Provisional Sanctuaries at @tashkeelstudio, Dubai. Shamin Sharum on the small prayer rooms in UAE warehouses and workplaces. Until 27 November

Two of these close in November, so don't leave them too late.
