## 1. خبر امروز / Today's story

### Facts used (all from sources listed below)
- Frieze Abu Dhabi runs **19 to 22 November 2026** at **Manarat Al Saadiyat**. The first day is by invitation; public days start **20 November**.
- It is described as the **first Frieze fair in the Middle East**. Abu Dhabi Art's **final edition was in 2025**.
- **Dyala Nusseibeh**, who led Abu Dhabi Art, continues as director (The National).
- **Frieze Sculpture Abu Dhabi**: **16 November to 16 December**, **Mamsha Beach, Saadiyat Island**. Artists: **Zeinab Alhashemi** (The Nut and Bolt), **Mohamed Ahmed Ibrahim** (The Form), **Imran Qureshi** (Water Bodies), **Kamran Samimi** (Voyagers (Traveling Stones)).

### Also this week
- **Public Art Abu Dhabi Biennial** (2nd edition), theme "Home: A Glossary for a Communal Sense of Place", artistic director Elvira Dyangani Ose. Opened in Al Ain 23 October, expands to Abu Dhabi city from **15 November**, runs until 15 February 2027.
